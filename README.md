# WICKFALL

A 2D top-down pixel-art action RPG made in **Unity 6 (URP 2D)** for Windows, in French and English.
You are the last Wickbearer, a candle golem carrying a shard of the dead Grand Foyer.
**Wax = life = light = risk:** shining lets you see, but it also lets the dark see you.

Design source: `Wickfall_Idee_Complete.pdf` (copy and transcript in `Docs/Source/`).

## Play it
- **Windows build:** `Builds/Windows/Wickfall.exe` (make it with `WICKFALL > Build > Windows`).
- **In Unity 6000.2.7f2:** open `Assets/Scenes/Boot.unity` → Play. Any level scene also plays on its own.
- **How to play, level by level:** `Docs/HOW_TO_PLAY.md`
- **Gameplay videos (a bot playing each region):** `Builds/Videos/`

## What is in it
- 6 regions: Champs de Suif, Bois-Mèche, Fonderies de Suie, Cathédrale de Verre, Creux Profond and Chambre du Foyer.
- 5 flames, 6 relics, 4 pacts, 10 creature kinds and the Roi Creux (3 phases).
- 3 endings: A, B and the secret C.
- Light puzzles, hazards, braziers, a shop, scores and ranks, and region select.
- Encrypted saves, options (including brightness), and French or English.
- Every number from the PDF is checked by tests. A bot finishes every region and reaches every ending.

## Build and test (Unity closed)
```
powershell -File Tools/unity.ps1 -Method Wickfall.EditorTools.WickfallSetup.Run   # rebuild generated content
powershell -File Tools/unity.ps1 -Tests EditMode
powershell -File Tools/unity.ps1 -Tests PlayMode -Filter Wickfall.Tests.Region1Tests
powershell -File Tools/unity.ps1 -Method Wickfall.EditorTools.WickfallBuild.BuildWindows
```
Art, levels and sound are generated by scripts in `Tools/` (Python 3 with numpy and Pillow).
