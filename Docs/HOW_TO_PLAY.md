# How to play WICKFALL — level by level

## The basics (read this first)
- **Your wax = your life = your light.** It goes down all the time. At 0 you go out and restart at the last brazier.
- **Get wax back:** rest at a **brazier** (E), drink from a **wax pool** (E), pick up the drops enemies leave.
- **Every level is the same idea:** find the **Great Bell**, ring it (E), survive the creatures it wakes, then walk through the gate to the **exit**.
- **Optional:** the **relic** (a permanent power-up) and the **flame shrine** (a new flame).
- **Sneak:** hold **Shift** to trim your wick. Your light gets small and enemies see you much less.
- **Flames:** press **1–5** to switch (costs 8 wax). Some enemies only take damage from certain flames.

**Keys:** move WASD · attack J · flame art K · dodge Space · sneak Shift · interact E · pause Esc

---

## 1. Champs de Suif (wax fields)
- Rest at the brazier near the start.
- **Geysers** glow before they erupt. Wait for the burst, then walk past.
- **Driplings** are slow. Just hit them.
- South room: the **Blue flame** (shoots from far away).
- Far north-west: the **relic** (one extra dodge). Hold **Shift** to sneak past the group of Driplings on the way.
- The **bell** is in the east. Ring it, fight, then take the exit north.

## 2. Bois-Mèche (black forest)
- The dark under the trees **hides you**.
- **Flowers** eat your light for 3 seconds if your light touches them. Hold Shift and walk past.
- **Lurkers** only move while you shine. Hold Shift and they freeze.
- **Husks** hang from branches and drop when you get close. Be ready to hit them.
- West: the **relic** (lets you climb). You need it for the **vines** (E) that go up the cliff.
- North-east: the **Green flame**. Then go west to the **bell** and the exit.

## 3. Fonderies de Suie (factory)
- **Presses** slam down. Wait until one lifts, then run under it.
- **Belts** push you. **Steam vents** push you too.
- **Bellows** shoot soot (−10 wax). Hide behind pillars and hit them up close.
- **Slagborn** (big metal golems) only take damage from **Green or White**. Switch flame first!
- East room: the **White flame**. West: the **relic** (+20 max wax). Then the **bell** and the exit.

## 4. Cathédrale de Verre (glass cathedral)
- **Crystal pedestal:** press E to turn it until its beam hits the socket. The gate opens.
- **Lasers** show a thin line before they fire. Wait for them to stop.
- East: the **relic** (a 5th flame slot, so you can carry **Crimson**).
- **Crimson** breaks glass walls, but it also cracks **glass floors**. Switch away from Crimson before crossing the glass bridge.
- **Reflections** copy your last 3 moves. Attack, then step back.
- **The Rose:** turn its pedestal so the beam hits the Rose's centre. Then hit each of the 5 petals with **its own flame colour**. The gate opens, then go to the **bell** and the exit.

## 5. Creux Profond (total darkness)
- You only see where your light reaches.
- Wax drains **1.5× faster**, so keep moving and use the pools and the brazier.
- **Unlit** (black shapes) drift toward the brightest light. Burn them with **White**, or switch flame (the flash lures them away).
- Your wax drops on the floor leave a trail back.
- Route: east → north (the **relic**) → east (the brazier) → long dark path west → **bell** → exit.

## 6. Chambre du Foyer (the end)
- **Ash** slows you down.
- **Pact shrines:** a permanent bonus with a permanent cost. You can say no.
- West: the **relic**. East: a brazier. **Rest before the boss!**
- **Roi Creux (boss):**
  - Watch where he will **slam** and move out.
  - **Dodge** (Space) through his black orbs and the ash ring.
  - Use **White**: it hurts him the most.
  - Phase 2: you can't sneak. Phase 3: your max wax is 40.
- When he falls, press E: **spare** him or **put him out**.
- Walk to the **Foyer** (north) and choose your ending:
  - **A:** relight the Foyer.
  - **B:** keep your flame.
  - **C:** pass the wick on. Needs **all 6 relics** and the King **spared**.
