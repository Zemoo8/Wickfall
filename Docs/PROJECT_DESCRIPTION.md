## The idea
You are the last **Wickbearer**, a candle golem carrying a shard of the dead **Great Hearth**.
The world has gone dark, and the **Hollows**, voids shaped like things that were once lit, hunt
anything that shines. You must cross six regions and relight the Hearth.

**One resource: wax = life = light = risk.** Your wax is your health and also your light. It burns
down all the time, and every hit costs some. A big flame lets you see far and hit hard, but the
Hollows see you from far away too. Hold a key to **trim your wick**: your light shrinks and enemies
barely notice you, but you hit half as hard.

> **Golden rule: you can't be strong and hidden at the same time.**

## How it plays
- **Explore in the dark.** Your light is all you see. Rest at **braziers** (checkpoints) to refill
  your wax, drink from wax pools, and pick up the wax that defeated enemies drop.
- **Choose: shine or hide.** Sneak past creatures with a small flame, or burn bright and fight.
- **Five flames.** Amber (melee), Blue (ranged, slows), Green (poison, hits groups), White
  (anti-Hollow, huge light) and Crimson (stronger when your wax is low). Each creature is weak to
  some flames, and switching costs 8 wax and makes a flash that draws enemies.
- **Each region:** find the **Great Bell**, ring it to open the exit, and survive the creatures its
  toll wakes up. Optional: the region's **relic** (a permanent power) and its **flame shrine**.
- **Score and rank** at the end of each region: wax left, time, relics, enemies avoided, deaths.

## The six regions
1. **Tallow Fields** — a wax plain in low light; geysers that warn before they erupt.
2. **Wickwood** — a black forest whose canopy hides you; light-eating flowers, climbing vines.
3. **Soot Foundries** — conveyors, presses that slam on a rhythm, steam jets, armoured Slagborn.
4. **Glass Cathedral** — aim light beams with crystals to power a rose window; mirror Reflections
   copy your last moves.
5. **Deep Hollow** — no light at all; the map exists only where you shine.
6. **Hearth Chamber** — the **Hollow King**, a boss in three phases, then the final choice.

**Three endings:** A) relight the Hearth and die in it, B) keep the flame and stay alone,
C) (secret) pass the wick on to someone else — only with all 6 relics and the King spared.

## What is in the game
- 6 playable regions, 5 flames, 6 relics, 4 pacts, a shop, 10 kinds of creatures and the boss.
- Light puzzles, stealth based on light, hazards, braziers, scores and ranks, region select.
- Main menu, options (volume, brightness, display, language), how-to-play pages, credits.
- Encrypted save with a backup copy; keyboard, mouse and gamepad.

## How it is built
- **Unity 6 with URP 2D:** real 2D lights (the player's flame is a light), shadows from walls,
  post-processing (bloom, vignette).
- **Data-driven:** every number from the design PDF lives in ScriptableObjects, and a test checks
  that the shipped values match the PDF.
- **Clean architecture:** systems talk only through event channels; every creature and the player
  run on a small state machine; the pure game logic is plain C# with unit tests.
- **Performance:** object pools and allocation-free physics queries, so gameplay frames create no
  garbage.
- **Content made by code:** levels are text (ASCII) maps; the pixel art, the sound effects and the
  music are generated by Python scripts; editor scripts build the scenes and prefabs.
- **Tested:** 77 unit tests, plus play tests in which a bot plays and finishes every region and
  reaches every ending.
